Research Experience

CNSE, University at Albany, USA (Prof. Ji Ung Lee’s lab)
Topological superconductivity for fault-tolerant quantum computation

  • Electrical transport studies of topological superconductors in collaboration with Stony Brook University and BNL (Prof. Qiang Li and Prof. Genda Gu).
  • Developed processes for end-to-end fabrication of nanoscale quantum devices.
  • Upgraded a closed-cycle cryostat, designed and implemented cryogenic low-pass filters (LPFs) for precision electrical measurements, and developed Python scripts for instrument automation.
  • Demonstrated half-integer flux quantization, intrinsic magnetization of the superconducting condensate, and Aharonov-Bohm oscillations of edge states in mesoscopic Fe(Te,Se) devices.

Laboratory for nanotechnology, University of Tehran, Iran (Prof. Abdi’s lab)
CMOS-compatible solid-state devices based on 2D materials

  • Investigated charge transport across graphene-silicon 2D/3D Schottky junctions.
  • Developed a model for the fundamental efficiency of graphene-based solar cells.
  • Fabricated and characterized infrared detectors based on 2D MoS2 layers.
  • Developed scalable position-sensitive detectors based on hybrid organic/inorganic PEDOT:PSS/Silicon heterojunctions (APL Editor’s Pick).
  • Pioneered a scalable method for synthesis of 2D borophene metallic layers using chemical vapor deposition.

Department of Physics, University of Tehran (Prof. Abdi’s and Prof. Arzi’s joint group)

  • Fabrication and characterization of nanostructured solar cells based on dye-sensitized solar cells (solar simulator, IV, quantum efficiency, carrier lifetime and diffusion length measurement).
  • Monte-Carlo simulation of charge transport in disordered wide band-gap semiconductors for photovoltaic and optoelectronic applications.
  • Computer modeling for PECVD growth of single-standing carbon nanotubes.

Nanoelectronic laboratory, Tarbiat Modares University, Iran (Dr. Sara Darbari’s lab)

  • Investigated sound-driven CNT-based tribo-electric nanogenerator in sonic array.
  • Developed a piezo-phototronic device based on graphene/MoS2 heterostructure.
