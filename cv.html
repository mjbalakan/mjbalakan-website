Mohammad Javadi Balakan

Research Scientist · San Jose, CA · U.S. permanent resident

mjbalakan@gmail.com · www.mjbalakan.com · Google Scholar · ORCID 0000-0002-2117-6668

Research interests

Experimental condensed-matter physics of quantum materials and mesoscopic devices: topological superconductivity, flux quantization and phase-coherent transport for fault-tolerant quantum computing; earlier work on 2D-material and hybrid organic–silicon devices for optoelectronics and photovoltaics.
