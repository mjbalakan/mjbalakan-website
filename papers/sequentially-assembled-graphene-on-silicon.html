2019 · Advanced Optical Materials 7, 1900470

Sequentially assembled graphene layers on silicon, the role of uncertainty principles in graphene–silicon Schottky junctions

M. Javadi, A. A. Noroozi, A. Mazaheri, Y. Abdi
Journal
In brief

Electrons in an atom-thin sheet barely move out of the sheet, so the uncertainty principle links a 2D layer's thickness to how easily charge enters the material beneath it. We stacked uncoupled CVD graphene layers one by one on silicon to test this on a macroscopic device.

Key results
  • Sequentially assembled CVD graphene layers stay electronically uncoupled.
  • In-plane current scales with the number of assembled layers.
  • Layer thickness strongly modifies charge injection and the reverse saturation current of the graphene–silicon junction.
