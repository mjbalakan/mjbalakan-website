Summary

Experimental physicist with more than a decade of hands-on work taking quantum and semiconductor devices from design and cleanroom fabrication to low-noise cryogenic measurement. Recent work on topological superconductivity in Fe(Te,Se) nanoscale ring devices for fault-tolerant quantum computing, published in Physical Review B (Editors’ Suggestion). Earlier, CMOS-compatible 2D-material and hybrid organic–silicon optoelectronic devices. Now combining this experience with AI for science and technology.

Core skills

Device fabrication and structural characterization: cleanroom nanofabrication, process development, electron-beam lithography (EBL), focused ion beam (FIB), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), atomic force microscopy (AFM), reactive ion etching (RIE), Raman and optical spectroscopy, thin film deposition (PVD, e-beam evaporation, CVD, PECVD), 2D materials, wide bandgap oxides, organic semiconductors, topological insulators, and topological superconductors.

Electrical characterization: DC/AC semiconductor parameter analysis (SMU, LCR, etc.), optoelectronic characterization tests (photovoltaic cells, optical detectors/sensors), low-noise/precision measurements (lock-in amplifiers, Atto-sense modules, low-temperature LPFs), cryogenic systems, quantum transport, phase-sensitive magnetic/flux measurements.

Computation, data analysis, and automation: Python, Mathematica, LabVIEW, KLayout (layout/mask design), statistical analysis, instrument automation.
