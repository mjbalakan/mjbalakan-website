Research · 2022 – present

Topological superconductivity for quantum computing

Topological superconductors can host Majorana modes, the building block of qubits that resist errors by design. I fabricate hBN-encapsulated rings of the iron-based superconductor Fe(Te,Se), a few hundred nanometres across, and measure them at a few kelvin with low-noise electronics.

  • Φ₀/2Half-quantum flux oscillations, the signature of half-integer phase winding
  • h/eCoherent electron interference along the edges of a superconducting ring
  • BeffA built-in magnetization of the superconducting condensate (PRB Letter, Editors' Suggestion)
2015 – 2021 · University of Tehran

Earlier: 2D materials and silicon devices

Before quantum devices I worked on combining 2D materials and polymers with silicon for sensors, solar cells and future chips, and on modeling how charge moves through disordered materials.
